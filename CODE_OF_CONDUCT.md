# Code of conduct

ASM BOTS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and on asmbots.io (handles, bot names, bot sources, and tournament names).

In short: be kind to people, and ruthless only to their bots. Harassment, slurs, doxxing, and sexual content are not allowed anywhere, including in a bot's `%name`, `%strategy`, or comments.

## Reporting

Email **pedram.amini@gmail.com** with `ASM BOTS conduct` in the subject. Reports are private. The maintainer answers each one and acts on it as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines say: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. On asmbots.io, that can also mean renaming or removing a bot or a handle.
