# Imp gates

## The problem

A bomber with a stride of 3 or more leaves gaps, and an imp moves through the gaps: it writes its next word over any bomb ahead of it. So a dwarf ties imps it should beat, and a bot that ties everything scores points on a hill. An imp gate is a cheap answer: one word of the core that the bot keeps changing, on the path an imp must take.

## How a gate kills

An imp built on `movsw` and `nop` walks up the core, so it meets a word under a bot's body before the body. The gate is such a word: the bot decrements it again and again, as fast as it can.

The imp writes its next word, then runs it two cycles later (see [imps](/docs/strategy/imps#why-an-imp-is-hard-to-kill)). If the gate's `dec` lands in between, the imp runs a word one less than it wrote:

| the imp's word at the gate | after `dec` | runs as | then |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A5 90` (movsw, nop) | `A4 90` | `movsb`, `nop` | copies 1 byte, not 2: the imp runs into a half-written word and dies in the gap |
| `90 A5` (nop, movsw) | `8F A5` | `pop` with ModR/M `A5` | `8F /4` is undefined: it dies at once |

Which row happens depends on where the imp started: an imp whose words are one byte out of step with the gate meets the second row. Either way the imp dies under the body.

## The gate

```asm run="vs=imp"
; Gate runs two processes: a gate that decrements a word 16 bytes under its body on eight turns
; in nine, and a bomber that walks down the core and drops a DAT word every 9 bytes.
; An imp walks up the core, so it reaches the gate before the body, and it runs each word two
; turns after it writes it: a decrement in between makes movsw a movsb, or nop an undefined pop,
; and the imp dies in the gap under the body.
; The bomber is the dwarf's loop unrolled to four bombs a pass, with a wide stride that laps the
; core fast, and an imp that it misses still dies at the gate, so imps and imp rings seldom tie.
; vs imp.asm, seeds 1..20: 20 W / 0 T / 0 L
; vs dwarf.asm, seeds 1..20: 14 W / 0 T / 6 L

%name     "Gate"
%author   "ASM Bots"
%strategy "Stop imps at the gate, bomb the core"

GATE    equ     16                      ; the gate word is this far under the base
STRIDE  equ     9                       ; bytes between bombs, which never land on the gate
PASS    equ     4 * STRIDE              ; bytes a pass covers: four bombs
SIZE    equ     end - start
LAP     equ     (0x10000 - SIZE) / PASS ; passes in a lap: the last bomb lands past the body

; Setup: the base idiom puts our base address in bx, ax = 0 is the bomb, and spl starts the bomber.
start:  call    .here
.here:  pop     bx
        sub     bx, .here
        xor     ax, ax
        spl     bomb

; Gate: decrement the gate word on eight turns in nine, forever.
gate:   times   8 dec word [bx-GATE]
        jmp     gate

; Bomb: each lap starts under our base and walks down, four bombs a pass.
bomb:   lea     di, [bx-STRIDE]
        mov     cx, LAP
.bomb:  mov     [di], ax
        mov     [di-STRIDE], ax
        mov     [di-2*STRIDE], ax
        mov     [di-3*STRIDE], ax
        sub     di, PASS
        loop    .bomb
        jmp     bomb

end:
```

`times 8 dec word [bx-GATE]` is 8 copies of one 3-byte instruction, so the gate process decrements on 8 of its 9 turns; the ninth is the `jmp`. The gate and the bomber share the bot's turn, so the gate word changes on 8 cycles in 18. An imp's word sits on the gate for 2 cycles, and a `dec` nearly always lands in them.

The bomber's first bomb is at bx - 9 and the pattern steps down by 9, so bombs land 9, 18, 27 bytes under the base: never on the gate at 16.

| seeds 1..20, hill rules | record |
|---|---|
| `gate` vs `imp` | 20 W / 0 T / 0 L |
| `gate` vs `imp-ring` | 18 W / 2 T / 0 L |
| `gate` vs `dwarf` | 14 W / 0 T / 6 L |
| `imp` vs `gate` | 0 W / 1 T / 19 L |

<Note>

Try replacing `spl bomb` with `jmp bomb`. The gate process never starts, and Gate is a plain bomber at stride 9. Against the imp the record falls from 20 W / 0 T / 0 L to 15 W / 5 T / 0 L, and against the imp ring from 18 W / 2 T / 0 L to 9 W / 5 T / 6 L. A slower gate still works: with `times 1` in place of `times 8`, it scores 18 W / 2 T / 0 L against the imp.

</Note>

## What a gate costs

- **A process.** The gate takes half of the bot's turns, so the bomber runs at half speed.
- **Imps only.** The gate guards the way an imp comes: up, from under the body. Bombs, scans, and copies come from anywhere, and the gate does nothing about them.
- **Its own code.** `times 8` is 24 bytes, a target for bombs and a find for scanners. `times 1` is 3.

See also: [imps and imp rings](/docs/strategy/imps), and [`dec`](/docs/reference/arithmetic#dec) in the reference.
